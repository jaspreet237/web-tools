About GST Late Fee & Interest Calculations

Under GST laws, filing returns after the prescribed due date attracts a late fee split equally between CGST and SGST, alongside interest charges calculated on delayed cash tax payments.

Key Rules Handled

• Auto Due Dates: The system automatically evaluates standard statutory dates (e.g., 20th for GSTR-3B, 11th/13th for GSTR-1, April 30th for GSTR-4, Dec 31st for GSTR-9) based on the selected tax period.
• Interest on Delayed Payment: Interest is calculated at 18% per annum under Section 50 on net tax liability cleared through cash ledger.
