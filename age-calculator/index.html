Age Calculator

Our free age calculator calculates your exact age based on your date of birth. The result shows your age in years, months and days and also provides additional information such as total days, total weeks and your next birthday.

How to use the Age Calculator

Enter your date of birth, select the date on which you want to calculate your age, and click "Calculate Age". If you select today's date, the calculator will show your current age.

Is this Age Calculator free?

Yes. The MyAllCodes Age Calculator is completely free to use and does not require registration. Your date is processed directly in your browser.
