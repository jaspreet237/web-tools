Time Offset Reference

• IST to EDT (Daylight Saving Time): IST is 9 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Eastern Daylight Time (EDT) from March to November.
• IST to EST (Standard Time): IST is 10 hours and 30 minutes ahead of Eastern Standard Time (EST) from November to March.
