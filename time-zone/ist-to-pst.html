Time Offset Reference

• IST to PST: IST is 11:30 hours ahead of Pacific Daylight Time (PDT) and 12:30 hours ahead of Pacific Standard Time (PST).
• GMT to IST: IST is consistently 5 hours 30 minutes ahead of GMT/UTC.
